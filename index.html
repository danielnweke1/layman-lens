How Layman Lens Works

1

Upload your Jargon

Paste any dense medical record, legal contract, or academic paper. Or, use the Upload PDF button to import massive documents.

2

Choose your Audience

Use the Audience Slider in the Lens. Want the explanation tuned for a 5-year-old? Or a College Grad? You have full control.

3

Highlight & Decode

Highlight any confusing phrase in the editor. Click the glowing Explain button that pops up to decode the complex instantly.

4

Magic Rewrite

Like the simple version better? Click "Replace in Document" to permanently swap out the confusing jargon with plain English.
